## Curriculum Rule

The roadmap should prioritize:

**Foundation → Core Analyst Skills → Data Cleaning & Transformation →
Analysis → Visualization/BI → Business Analytics → Projects → Interview
→ Job Readiness**

Advanced topics should support the learner's career path without
overwhelming the core entry-level curriculum.
